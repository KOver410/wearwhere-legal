Account & Data Deletion

WearWhere — Last updated: June 30, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your WearWhere account and the personal data associated with it. You can delete your account directly in the app, or send us a request by email.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

  1. Open the WearWhere app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm by entering your password.

Your account is immediately deactivated and is permanently deleted within 90 days. You will not be able to sign in after submitting the request.

If you have orders that are still being processed or shipped, please wait until they are completed (or cancel them) before deleting your account.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, send a deletion request to ngoduyanhkover@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Please include:

  • Your full name as registered in the app
  • The email address and/or phone number used for your account

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before processing the request. We will action verified requests within 90 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove or anonymize:

  • Your account and profile information (name, email, phone number, password, profile photo, bio)
  • Saved shipping addresses and contact details
  • Your outfit posts (OOTD), wardrobe items, wishlists, and reviews
  • Content you submitted to our AI styling features

What we may keep

Some records are retained for a limited period to comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations, to resolve disputes, and to prevent fraud:

DataReasonRetention
Order & transaction records (anonymized where possible) Legal, tax, and accounting requirements; dispute resolution As required by applicable law, then deleted or kept anonymized

Payment instrument data (card numbers, bank credentials) is never stored on our servers — payments are handled by our provider, PayOS.

Questions

For anything related to your data, contact us at ngoduyanhkover@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
