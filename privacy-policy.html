Privacy Policy

WearWhere — Last updated: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WearWhere ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the WearWhere mobile application and related services (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password, and (optionally) profile photo and bio.
  • Sign-in data: if you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google account profile (name, email, profile picture) as permitted by Google.
  • Shipping & contact details: delivery addresses and recipient information you save in your address book.
  • Order information: products purchased, order history, vouchers used, and related transaction details.
  • User-generated content: outfit posts ("OOTD"), wardrobe items, wishlists, reviews, and messages you submit to our AI styling features.

1.2 Payment information

Payments are processed by our third-party payment provider, PayOS. When you make a purchase, your payment is handled directly by PayOS. We do not collect or store your full card numbers, bank credentials, or other sensitive payment instrument data on our servers. We receive only the transaction result and a payment reference needed to fulfil your order.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage data: device model, operating system, app version, language, and basic interaction logs used to operate and improve the App.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data to help us fix problems.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • PayOS — to process payments.
  • Shipping & logistics partners — to deliver your orders (name, address, phone number).
  • AI service providers — to power our AI styling and chat features; content you submit may be processed by these providers to generate responses.
  • Brands / merchants on the platform — order details needed to fulfil purchases from them.
  • Service providers — hosting, analytics, and crash-reporting vendors that act on our behalf.
  • Legal & safety — authorities when required by law, or to protect our rights, users, and the public.

4. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal information (most data is editable in the App's profile and address settings).
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Withdraw consent or object to certain processing.

To exercise these rights, or to request account deletion, contact us at ngoduyanhkover@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. International Users

The App is operated from Vietnam. If you access the App from outside Vietnam, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Vietnam or in countries where our service providers operate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by notice within the App.
